We travel thinking we are searching for places.

In reality, we are searching for connections.

Mountains, cities, deserts, and oceans become the backdrop of something far more meaningful: the moments when two souls meet and recognize one another.

What is Fragments of the Soul?

Fragments of the Soul is a collection of encounters.

A celebration of the awe of human connection that transcends borders, cultures, language, space, and time.
